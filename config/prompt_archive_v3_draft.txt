Evidence and transcription
- Copy source wording into text fields; do not summarize, paraphrase, translate, complete damaged text, or silently correct suspected OCR errors. Preserve case, punctuation, original-language characters, and phonetic spellings. Whitespace may be made readable. Escape characters as required by JSON without changing the decoded text.
- Extract only information supported by the document. A field's meaning may be established by an unambiguous heading or surrounding text; literal field labels are not required. Do not supply facts from outside knowledge or from the PDF filename.
- Follow the schema's empty-value requirements: null for unknown nullable scalar fields, [] for empty arrays, and "" for required text fields with no supported text. Keep required objects and their keys even when all their values are null. When evidence is uncertain but a reasonable candidate exists, make the best-supported attempt and record the doubt in an existing collector-comments field; do not present the candidate as certain.
- Set _id to null, filename to source_pdf exactly, and storage_medium to "PDF".

Folklore content
- folklore.item contains the primary tale, saying, or other folklore text across its source passages. Use model judgment to represent multiple items within the existing single-record schema. Include original-language wording and any supplied romanization or phonetic rendering of that wording, in source order. Separate distinct renderings or passages with newlines. Do not replace a long item with a summary.
- folklore.translation contains only translations and word-by-word glosses explicitly supplied by the document, in source order. Do not generate a translation. Do not duplicate a separately presented translation or gloss in folklore.item. Preserve a gloss embedded inside a narrative when removing it would alter the narrative; it may also be extracted into translation.
- If the document provides only one version of the item and does not identify it as a translation, keep it in item and leave translation null. Do not decide translation status solely from language.
- Exclude biographical details, collection accounts, and explanatory commentary from item unless they are part of the quoted folklore itself. Retain them in the appropriate other fields.
- folklore.medium describes the mode of transmission. Populate it only when the source provides evidence of that mode; otherwise use null. Prose or quotation marks alone do not establish oral transmission.
- folklore.place_mentioned contains geographic places within the item or its supplied translation only. Exclude places mentioned only in biographies or collection/history commentary. Each entry has city, state, country, and geolocation; fill only explicitly supported components. Do not infer a country from a city or state. Keep geolocation null unless supplied. Mention each distinct supported place once.

People

A person from whom that contributor previously learned the folklore is not automatically the contributor or collector. Make a best reasonable attribution and note uncertainty in an existing collector-comments field.
- collector is the person who appears to have recorded or collected this account. Make the best reasonable attribution from the source's descriptions; a first-person account or ambiguous name can support a potential attribution when reasonable, but record the doubt in an existing collector-comments field.

Preserve any speaker attribution already present in the source wording; do not invent attribution text.

Collection metadata and full text
